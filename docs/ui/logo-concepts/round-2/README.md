# L2 — Friendly, rounded logo concepts

[Open gallery](index.html) · [1440px preview](preview-1.png)

Six independent directions, each supplied as 48×48 SVG, dark SVG and Ice app icon
with rx=12. No raster art, text inside symbols, gradients, dependencies or external
requests. Maximum three brand colors per symbol. Navy becomes Ice in dark variants;
blue-only concepts retain their colors. Outfit loads from the existing repo font.
Keep the repository structure when opening the HTML locally.

| Concept | Idea | Strength | Weakness at 24px |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 [Hug check](01-hug-check.svg) | A generous C hugs a small agreement check, with a soft inner crescent. | Warm, expressive C with a clear focal point. | Inner crescent becomes a highlight at 24px. |
| 02 [Seal check](02-seal-check.svg) | A soft circular impression opens on the right around a small checked center. | Bold, self-contained silhouette. | The nested shapes are denser than the other options. |
| 03 [Dot C](03-dot-c.svg) | One rounded C surrounds the dot representing a deal on record. | Very simple and memorable at small sizes. | The dot can suggest a generic target. |
| 04 [Two halves](04-two-halves.svg) | Two curved parties meet around a shared open center. | Friendly two-party motion without a handshake illustration. | The separation may look like a broken C at 24px. |
| 05 [Receipt C](05-receipt-c.svg) | The lower arm of a C rolls into a soft receipt edge. | Connects the initial to a recorded agreement. | The small receipt ripple needs close inspection at 24px. |
| 06 [Record pebble](06-record-pebble.svg) | A rounded folded ribbon keeps one bright dot in its curved pocket. | Distinctive organic pocket and recorded-deal dot. | Pocket meaning is abstract without the wordmark. |

## Top two

1. **01 Hug check** — the embracing gesture adds personality while keeping the C
   and agreement check easy to explain. Best direction to refine with the owner.
2. **03 Dot C** — the clearest small-size mark; a single dot gives “on record” a
   memorable visual anchor without detailed decoration.

Round 1 was reviewed and rejected by the owner as rigid and template-like. This
round uses thick curved strokes and round caps, not its squared silhouettes.
No sheet-1.jpg or sheet-2.jpg mood references were present. All geometry was drawn
for this study; no real company symbol was traced. The seal is a soft record
impression, without currency motifs, ridges or crypto styling. No option selected.
